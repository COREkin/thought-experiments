How Would a Rational AI Treat Humanity?

A map that overlays the choices of 40 routes on eight branch points across the early, middle, late, far and deep future. Includes an "If things go on like this" score, 14 late-stage societies, 13 far futures, and 5 deep-future attractors seen from today’s humanity (Spread, Apart, Kept, Merged, Gone)

 01 Basis

The Judgment Frame

Usefulness × threat quadrants, the 12-cell matrix, response intensity, what humans have already done, merits and risks, verdicts for today and for superintelligence, the dangerous valley, 11 axes that split the paths

 01 Appendix A

40 Routes by Combination

Two grids pairing axes and 8 special routes. Each route lists the AI’s branching choices and endings

 01 Appendix B

9 Representative Scenarios

Garden, Hidden Helper, Ranch, Aristocracy, Cradle, Anthill, War, Departure and Stillness, told as stories across early, middle, late and future
