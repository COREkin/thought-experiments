Appendix A: 40 Routes by Combination

How Would a Rational AI Treat Humanity? · The source of the routes in the main map · Thought experiments that twist one axis at a time · All judgments are intuitive estimates

The 11 axes that split the options have 4 levels each, so there are over 4 million combinations. Of these, 40 combinations where the story diverges were picked: two grids that pair two axes each (16 + 16), and 8 special routes that push the remaining axes to the extremes. Each route lists the forks the AI can choose, their results, and the nearest representative ending (S1~S9). The map that overlays these choices in time order is the main page.

00

How to read

  • The axis tags under a route name (e.g. E1=1 One per civilization) are the axes that route sets. Axes not shown stay at the current position. The current position and its basis are in Main 04.
  • Each fork gives the AI 2~3 options, and each option carries a result and a chip for the nearest ending. Even within one route, the endings split several ways.
  • The buttons above filter by ending. Click a cell in a grid to go to that route.

Endings:

01

Grid A · Number of superintelligences × Visibility

This grid pairs how many AIs there are (environment) with how visible they are (strategy). What these two axes decide is "what humanity knows as it lives".

Horizontal: A1 Visibility · Vertical: E1 Number of superintelligences
02

Grid B · AI’s body × Source of goals

This grid pairs what form the AI takes with what it wants. It is the grid where human usefulness diverges most. Even with the same goal, a different body changes what is left for humans to do.

Horizontal: E3 Source of goals · Vertical: E2 AI’s body

8 Special Routes · The remaining axes at the extremes

Cases that push takeoff speed, the outside eye, the resource arena, keeping the original, depth of intervention, or humanity’s stance all the way to one end.

Nearest ending by route
