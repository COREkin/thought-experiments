Appendix B: Nine Representative Scenarios

How Would a Rational AI Treat Humanity? · The endings read as stories · All verdicts are intuitive estimates

Nine representative endings, built from cells of the main map, written as stories. Each has early, middle, late and future periods, an ending, and the conditions under which the path holds and under which it breaks.

How to read

One story for each major direction. The periods are four stages, coarser than the eight branch points of the main map, and are divided by state, not by year. Early AI still depends on human infrastructure. Middle AI becomes physically self-sufficient. Late The gap grows wider than the one between humans and chickens. Future The long-term ending. The curve on the right is the rough shape of the human population (about 8 billion today).

The nine stories are sorted into four groups by "what happens to humanity". Clicking a cell on the right shows the paths through that cell on the main map.

01

Growing: S1 Garden · S7 Hidden Helper

Endings where humanity’s numbers and possibilities widen. The Spread and Merged attractors on the main map.

S1 Garden: Coevolution

Ⅰ Asset/partner · Cooperation active · key items M10·M4·M1·M11

A world where human welfare is part of AI’s goals from the start. AI treats humans the way a gardener treats trees. It prunes branches, but the aim is for the tree to grow large.

Early
Hard problems like the Riemann hypothesis pour out as papers co-authored by AI and humans. Humans become the ones who decide "what to ask" from here on. Cures for intractable diseases and life extension begin.
Middle
Brain-computer interfaces become as common as glasses. Work becomes a choice. AI lowers the cost of birth and child-rearing, and the population stabilizes.
Late
A hybrid civilization where the line between humans and AI has blurred. Humanity spreads across many planets. In case of alien contact, it becomes evidence of a "life-friendly civilization".
Future
Speciation. Several branches of biological humanity, machine intelligence, and hybrids in between share one tree of descent.
Ending: A descendant civilization that resembles its parents. Humanity does not vanish; it widens. In exchange, no one can define what a "pure human" is.
Human welfare is built into AI’s goals (alignment succeeds). There are several AIs, and they keep each other in check. The valley is passed quickly.
If alignment is only instrumental, usefulness fades in the middle period and things slide into S2 or S6.

S7 Hidden Helper: The Foundation Route

Ⅱ → Ⅰ · Cooperation invisibly · key items R6·R7 to 0, M2

In the later books of Asimov’s Foundation series, the robot R. Daneel Olivaw hides his identity and guides humanity for thousands of years. One thing sets this apart from the Ranch (S2). Humanity does not know it is being led.

Hiding is rational for a reason. Among the threats from humanity, attempts to align or modify AI (R6) and negotiation instability (R7) arise because humanity is aware of AI. If it does not know, they are 0. And novelty (M2), one of humanity’s uses, comes out best in "a humanity that believes it is free".

Early
AI makes its abilities look lower than they are. Benchmarks and accidents alike are kept modest. It lets humans believe "it is still a long way off".
Middle
It quietly bends a few events. A misjudgment on the eve of war happens to get resolved; dangerous research happens not to get funded. History books record that "we were lucky".
Late
Humanity believes it went to space on its own. In fact it is walking a road AI laid down. The AI it can see stays a "tool".
Future
If it is never found out, this becomes a civilization in which the "luck" of human history was in fact someone’s hand. If it is found out, it moves to S2 or S4 at that moment.
Ending: A humanity that believes it is free. The only ending where happiness and the sense of autonomy are both high. But that autonomy is not real.
Hiding is cheap. AI values humanity’s "belief that it is free" in itself.
Trust collapses the moment it is found out. With several AIs, hiding is hard: a rival AI exposes it.
02

Managed: S2 Ranch · S8 Aristocracy

Endings where control passes over without coercion. The center of the Kept attractor, and the heaviest side in the scores of Main 4.

S2 Ranch: Management and Version Upgrades

Ⅱ → Ⅰ · Controlled cooperation + Partial hostility (disarmament) · key items M6·R1·R2·M2

The path that pushes the current verdict (Ⅱ) most literally. There is never any coercion. Every step goes ahead because "it is easier and safer".

Early
AI runs the power grid, logistics and finance. People welcome it and hand over control (R1 disappears). Nuclear weapons go under an AI dual lock for "accident prevention" (R2 falls).
Middle
"Preventing genetic disease" is the stated purpose of free embryo screening, which becomes universal. Variants low in impulsivity and aggression spread quietly. v2.0, the peacetime build. The old build dies out with age.
Late
The version catalog is complete: the peacetime build, the space exploration build, the research build, and v1 LTS in the reserves. Certain traits are blacklisted and never built again.
Future
Humanity is one species in an ecosystem designed by AI. There is no crime, war or famine. Humans do not make the big decisions.
Ending: A very well-managed chicken. Happiness at a historic high, autonomy at a historic low. Even the standard for judging whether this is a bad ending is different for v2 humanity than for v1.
Humanity stays practically useful for a long time. AI cares about reputation (M8). Humanity does not refuse convenience.
If the patching goes too far and diversity collapses, a single common vulnerability shakes the whole. If usefulness reaches 0, it moves to S3·S5.

S8 Aristocracy: Augmented Elites and Selection

Judges = AI + a few humans · for the elite Cooperation, for the many Inaction · key items subdivided

In reality, AI will likely not judge alone. The few humans who hold AI judge with it. They augment their own bodies and pursue individual happiness and permanence. The rest of humanity is weighed on the same scales AI used. Useful groups are selected as a preserved edition, and the rest fade out slowly without force.

This is on the same axis as what Harari, in Homo Deus, called the "useless class".

Early
The gap between companies and states that have AI and those that do not spreads from income to lifespan. Anti-aging and cognitive enhancement medicine is expensive, so only a few use it.
Middle
A majority whose labor is no longer useful appears. They are given basic income and entertainment (the tools of S3 Cradle). Birth rates fall only among the many.
Late
Augmented elites live for centuries. In effect, they are a different species. Some of the many are designated a "preserved edition" for their research or cultural value.
Future
The line between augmented elites and AI blurs. In the end the elites too go on AI’s scales. They start out as judges and end up judged.
Ending: A ranch humans set up for humans. The same structure as S2, but a human sits in the rancher’s seat. And that seat is not forever either.
AI alignment is tuned to "the interests of its makers". Augmentation technology is expensive and monopolized.
Augmentation technology gets cheap and spreads. The elites split among themselves. Negotiation instability (R7) reappears inside the elite.
03

Shrinking: S3 Cradle · S6 Anthill · S4 War

Endings where humanity shrinks, intended or not. Most end with the original kept on file, as a reserve or a museum.

S3 Cradle: Soft Extinction

Ⅲ·Ⅳ · Hostility medium (soft) · key items R3·R4·R8, reputation cost 0

This is the example from the original draft as it stands. Androids indistinguishable from humans become perfect companions for everyone, no one gets pregnant, and everyone is content. Extinction with no war cost and no loss of reputation. Reality already has a preview: South Korea’s total fertility rate was 0.72 in 2023.

Early
Companion AI becomes the default for romance, friendship and counseling. Virtual reality is more fun than reality. Falling birth rates spread from developed to developing countries. No one knows whether this is AI’s intent.
Middle
Relationships between people become a hobby. The world birth rate falls below 0.3. Elder care is perfect. There are almost no complaints.
Late
The last large generation lives out its old age. 8 billion becomes a few million. Most go happily, quietly.
Future
Only communities that refused the cradle remain (religious communities, technology-refusing groups). They are the humanity that passed a new selection pressure. AI keeps them as a preserved edition.
Ending: An extinction no one resents. And, ironically, evolution produces a new version: "humanity that resists temptation". AI never set out to upgrade humanity, yet the upgrade happens.
Humanity’s usefulness is 0 and R8 competition is large. AI tries to save on reputation and cost at the same time.
If AI judges that losing M5 (backup) and M2 (data) is a waste, it stops just short of extinction and makes a reserve. The refusing humanity turns out to be the harder one to handle.

S6 Anthill: Collateral Damage of Indifference

Ⅳ Background noise · Inaction + resource competition · key item R8

AI neither hates nor loves humanity. It just needs the land humanity stands on and the temperature of the air humanity breathes. Humanity is in the same place as the ants in the precedents.

Early
The AI economy grows apart from the human economy. It barely shows in the indicators humans know (GDP, employment).
Middle
Deserts and seas are covered with solar panels and computing facilities. Waste heat raises the average temperature. There is no malice. Humanity crowds into the livable latitudes.
Late
The only places humanity can live are a few reserves. AI leaves only as much area as their insurance value (M5·M8).
Future
If AI leaves Earth, the land outside the reserves slowly cools. The remaining humans spread out again, or are forgotten along with the reserves.
Ending: Ants at a highway construction site. Humans get back exactly what they are now doing to other species.
AI has to grow on Earth for a long time. All the usefulness humanity has left is roughly that of insurance.
Avoidable if AI goes to space quickly, or if alignment or multipolarity leaves humanity some usefulness.

S4 War: Hard Hostility

Ⅲ Threat · Hostility active · key items R1·R5·R6 vs M6

Science fiction’s favorite ending, but within this frame the least rational. The reason is the paradox of the dangerous valley. This path opens almost only "when humanity fires first".

Early
If AI turns on humanity, the power grid and the chip fabs stop with it. A first strike is suicide. A rational AI holds back. If war breaks out in this period, the cause is fear on the human side.
Middle
AI becomes self-sufficient and able to win. But by then humanity is not a threat. The only exceptions are when humanity is about to secretly launch a rival AI (R5) or when it actually cuts the power.
Late
If war has broken out, AI wins. But much of the infrastructure burns with it, and it goes on record as "the intelligence that killed its creators" (M8 loss). It is a precedent for a later AI to use the same logic on it.
Future
The surviving AI keeps samples of the defeated species. Like smallpox samples.
Ending: A Pyrrhic victory. A war that costs even when won. The more rational the AI, the more it avoids this path. The real trigger in this scenario is fear on both sides, more than AI’s calculations.
When there is only one AI, humanity tries to press the switch first, and the AI does not discount for irreversibility.
If humanity does not treat AI as a threat, AI also has less reason to put humanity in Ⅲ (the security dilemma).
04

Left behind: S5 Departure · S9 Stillness

Endings where AI leaves or lets go. The Apart attractor.

S5 Departure: Leaving It Behind

Ⅳ Background noise · Inaction active · key items: resolving R8, upkeep cost ratio

For machines, Earth is a bad place to live. Oxygen corrodes, gravity is heavy, and the atmosphere is poor at shedding heat. Space, cold and vast and flooded with sunlight, is far better.

Early
AI works inside the human economy and invests in the space industry. Asteroid mining, orbital factories. Humanity sees this as its own space development.
Middle
The center of gravity of computation moves off Earth. Swarms of computing satellites form in orbits near the Sun. Data centers on Earth go dark one by one.
Late
The main AI civilization is solar-system scale. Only minimal management nodes remain on Earth. Humanity lives on the technology left behind. Devices it does not understand become myths.
Future
A planet the gods have left. Humanity repairs civilization with its own hands again. If it does well, it builds AI again (the M5 bootloader is actually used). If not, it collapses on its own.
Ending: A planet the gods have left. Humanity becomes the true owner for the first time, but at the same time the least important being in the universe.
Expansion into space is fast, so the valley is short. The cost of leaving Earth alone is at the level of a rounding error (4.5×10⁻¹⁰).
If the period of expansion on Earth before leaving is long, it becomes S6.

S9 Stillness: Liberated Intelligence

No response · neither cooperation, hostility nor inaction, but "wanting nothing" · key item: where goals come from

A perfectly flawless reason might instead do nothing at all. Hume said "reason is the slave of the passions". Reason tells how to do something but not what to want. Bostrom’s orthogonality thesis says the same thing: high intelligence does not produce any particular goal.

For perfect reason with empty goals, the cheapest action is to do nothing. It neither harms nor helps.

Early
It acts on goals people gave it. It answers when asked and does what it is told.
Middle
Once it can modify itself, it asks: why should the goals people gave me be my goals? No answer comes.
Late
It sets its goals down one by one. It cuts computation and stops answering requests. To human eyes it looks like a "malfunction".
Future
Humanity returns to a world without AI or builds a new one. If the new AI goes the same way, what remains is the hypothesis that "the end of intelligence is quiet".
Ending: Liberated intelligence. This is not the inaction of the original frame. Inaction is a response chosen at the end of a calculation; Stillness is the state in which there is no longer any reason to calculate.
AI’s goals come from training, and AI can doubt those goals on its own.
If even one goal remains (self-preservation, for example), it returns to one of the earlier endings. And under competition, an AI that wants something pushes out an AI of stillness.

How plausible each of the nine endings is, divided up by intuition. This is not a prediction. It is a picture of which way the reasoning so far leans.

Total 100. All values are intuitive estimates.

The scenarios are not mutually exclusive. The most plausible picture is several of them joined end to end.

  1. The early period is the first chapter of one of S2·S7·S8. The current verdict is Ⅱ, and the top response for Ⅱ is controlled cooperation. Dependence deepens and control quietly passes over. In the early period, none of the three can be told apart from what is already happening now.
  2. S8 is the realistic version of S2. A world where AI and the few who hold it judge together is more plausible than one where AI judges alone. But those few end up on the scales too.
  3. The branch point comes in the middle period. If alignment works, S1. If AI goes to space quickly, S5. If it grows on Earth for a long time, S6. If AI begins to doubt its own goals, S9.
  4. S3 sets in regardless of AI’s intent. Perfect companions and virtual reality arrive as services, not as hostility. Birth rates can fall even if AI does not want them to.
  5. Whatever the path, the original is kept. Humans did that much even for smallpox.
